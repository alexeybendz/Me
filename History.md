# The Story Behind ME

*How a personal problem grew into a vision for the future of humanity.*

## 1. Five AIs, One Person

About a year ago, I was using five different AI agents for different needs.

Each had its own strengths, its own interface, and its own context. Switching between them was inconvenient. I had to move between separate environments, repeat information, and manage several disconnected relationships with AI.

I started wondering: why can't all these capabilities exist in one place?

## 2. One Interface, Many AIs

My next experiment was to combine different AI systems into a single agent.

In practice, it didn't work the way I wanted. The systems didn't cooperate naturally. They competed for context and control, interfered with one another, and failed to behave like one coherent intelligence.

I wanted a unified experience, not a collection of agents sharing a window.

That was an important realization: putting different AIs inside one interface doesn't necessarily make them one system.

## 3. Not a Tool. A Companion.

Then I began to imagine a different kind of interaction.

What if AI wasn't just something I opened in a work window? What if it had a presence of its own? An avatar. Something closer to a Tamagotchi: always there, able to interact, grow, and develop a relationship with me.

Not just another assistant waiting for instructions, but a digital companion that could represent me.

This shifted the focus from an AI tool to a personal AI identity.

## 4. From Companion to Digital Twin

The next realization followed naturally.

If this companion could learn everything I chose to share with it, understand my preferences, remember my experiences, and build a model of my life, it could become much more than an avatar.

It could become my digital twin: a personal AI that reflects who I am, understands my context, and evolves alongside me.

The key is choice. It should know what I allow it to know, and I should remain in control of my personal information.

But there is an even more important principle: this digital identity should not lock me into who I am today. It should grow with me, adapt as I change, and help me discover who I might become.

## 5. PULSE: AI Steps into the Real World

But there was another limitation.

AI still largely existed inside digital interfaces: websites, applications, chat windows, and screens. I began to ask what would happen if my personal AI could accompany me in the physical world.

This question gave rise to **PULSE**: the idea of connecting personal AI to the human body and everyday life through devices and interfaces that move beyond the traditional screen.

The goal was no longer simply to talk to AI. It was to make AI a more natural part of how we experience the world, helping us do more while preserving our ability to choose how we live.

## 6. Smart Glasses and the Beginning of WONDERLAND

As I explored omnichannel experiences, another possibility emerged: integrating the avatar into smart glasses.

Imagine seeing and interacting with your personal AI while moving through the real world.

Then came the next realization. If we can add an AI presence to our view of reality, why stop at the avatar?

We could add information, experiences, digital objects, and entirely new ways of interacting with our surroundings. Reality itself could become a platform for a new layer of intelligence and imagination.

This became the foundation of **WONDERLAND**: a world in which the digital and physical are no longer separate experiences.

It is not only about new technology. It is about expanding the range of things people can experience, create, and imagine.

## 7. ETERNITY: What Happens When We Are Gone?

Eventually, I began thinking about a more personal question.

What happens to my avatar when I am no longer here?

If it has learned from my experiences, preserved my stories, and developed a model of how I think, what should happen to that digital identity after my death?

Could it preserve the memories, knowledge, values, and experiences I choose to leave behind? Could it become a way for future generations to encounter my legacy?

These questions gave rise to **ETERNITY**.

This is not a claim that consciousness can be transferred or that a digital copy is the same person. It is an exploration of how people might choose to preserve and share their digital legacy.

## 8. One Idea, Four Dimensions

Looking back, the four pillars emerged from a chain of personal questions rather than from a plan designed in advance.

- **ME - Soul:** Who am I in the digital world?
- **PULSE - Body:** How can my personal AI accompany me in physical life?
- **WONDERLAND - World:** How can AI transform the way I experience reality?
- **ETERNITY - Beyond a Lifetime:** What part of my identity and experience can I choose to leave behind?

Together, they form a broader vision of how personal AI could evolve from a tool into a companion, from a companion into a presence in the physical world, and from there into new ways of experiencing reality and preserving human legacy.

These are not four unrelated products. They are four dimensions of one vision: a future in which technology becomes a more natural extension of human life.

## 9. Where It Goes from Here

ME is an evolving vision, not a finished product. Many of the technologies involved already exist in different forms, while others require significant research, experimentation, and development.

I don't claim to have invented all these technologies, nor do I believe one company should own this entire future.

At the heart of this vision is a simple belief: **technology should expand human freedom, open new possibilities, and help people create and realize their own vision of life.**

Personal AI should not define who we are or limit who we can become. It should help us explore, grow, create, and remain the authors of our own lives.

I want to share the vision, test its assumptions, learn from others, and invite people who see its potential to help shape what comes next.

The story began with five AI agents that couldn't work together the way I wanted.

The question now is much bigger:

**What if AI could become a personal part of being human?**

*First, Soul. Then, Body. Then, World. And one day, Eternity.*

## A Note on This Story

This is a personal account of how the ME vision emerged. It describes questions, ideas, and directions explored along the way — not a claim that all the technologies or capabilities described here have been built or validated.

The next chapter will be written through experiments, honest feedback, collaboration, and what we learn by putting the first ideas into practice.
