# ME — Project Status

**Current stage:** Early exploration and prototyping  
**Last updated:** October 2026

## What ME is

ME is an exploration of personal AI, digital identity, and continuity of personal context over time.

The long-term vision connects four dimensions:

- **ME** — personal digital identity and memory.
- **PULSE** — the connection between digital identity and the physical world.
- **WONDERLAND** — digital environments and immersive experiences.
- **ETERNITY** — the preservation of personal memories, knowledge, and legacy.

## What exists today

- The core vision and principles are documented.
- The project's conceptual architecture has been outlined.
- Technical experimentation is taking place in a separate private repository.

## What is being explored first

The initial hypothesis is that personal memory, connected experiences, and evolving context can make interactions with AI more useful.

The first priority is to understand and validate this hypothesis before expanding the scope.

## What is not yet claimed

- ME is not a finished product.
- The initial hypothesis has not yet been validated.
- The long-term architecture remains open to change.
- No claim is made that human consciousness can be transferred to a machine.

## Why this project is public

The public repository documents the vision, principles, and evolution of the idea.

It is a place to share the thinking behind ME, learn from feedback, and make the project's direction more transparent.

Technical prototypes remain private while the project is in its early stages.

## Next milestone

Clarify the first use case, define what meaningful improvement looks like, and identify a practical way to test the initial hypothesis.
